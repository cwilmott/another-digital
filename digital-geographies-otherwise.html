Another Digital is Possible

Digital Geographies, Otherwise?

AAG NYC 6-7 Feb 2027

Another Digital is Possible is a two-day conference that will take place in New York City on February 6-7, 2027. It will feature a mix of keynote speakers, panel discussions, workshops, and interactive sessions that explore the theme of "Another Digital Is Possible." The conference will bring together scholars, practitioners, and activists from a variety of disciplines to discuss the critical role of digital technologies in creating, perpetuating and resisting oppressive and unequal power structures. The conference will also provide opportunities for building solidarity and community among attendees.
